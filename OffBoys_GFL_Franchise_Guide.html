2013–2017

The Building Years

Two top-5 finishes, one 3rd place, but also two 8th-place showings and a brutal 11th. Kyle was learning the game, finding his footing in a competitive league, and laying the groundwork for what was to come. The record was 32-33 — hovering right at .500 — but the foundation was being built.

The Golden Era

1st, 5th, 3rd — the most consistent three-year run in franchise history, anchored by the 2018 Championship. Kyle went 26-13 across these three seasons, averaged 1,377 points scored, and cemented his legacy as a GFL Champion. This was the Off Boys at their absolute peak.

26-13-0 · Championship: 2018 · Avg Finish: 3.0

The Modern Era

Big scoring numbers but mixed results. A runner-up finish in 2022, a dark 2023 campaign, then the franchise's highest-scoring season ever in 2024 with 1,606 points. The Modern Era Off Boys can put up points with anyone — the challenge is translating firepower into postseason glory.
