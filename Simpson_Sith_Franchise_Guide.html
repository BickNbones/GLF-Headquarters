Franchise History

The Three Eras

Era I
Benton Hollow Blue
2012 – 2016  |  5 Seasons

Building the Foundation. The franchise's inaugural identity established Danny Sattler as a consistent competitor in the Gandorf National League. Anchored by veteran talent and steady management, the Blue era posted three winning records while learning the rhythms of championship contention. A combined 34-31 record over five seasons set the groundwork for what was to come.

Era II
NY Red Reapers
2017 – 2020  |  4 Seasons

The Reaper Ascension. A rebrand signaled a new era of aggression. The 2017 season stands as the Reapers' defining campaign — a 10-3 record and a 3rd-place finish that announced the franchise as a legitimate title threat. Speed and big-play weapons defined this era, headlined by Tyreek Hill and a supporting cast built for explosion. Combined record: 31-21.

Era III
Simpson Sith
2021 – 2025  |  5 Seasons

The Golden Era. The franchise's finest identity produced its most dominant season in 2022 — a historic 12-2 record, 1,653.16 points for, and a +360 differential that shook the league. Back-to-back 3rd-place finishes in 2022 and 2023 confirmed elite status. The Sith name carries weight: dark, calculated, and built to contend at the highest level.

Franchise Legacy

The Sattler Standard

A−

Danny Sattler's franchise history spans three identities and more than a decade of competition in the Gandorf National Fantasy Football League. From Benton Hollow Blue to NY Red Reapers to Simpson Sith, the organization continuously reinvented itself while remaining a perennial contender. The franchise reached its statistical peak during the Simpson Sith era, highlighted by the dominant 2022 campaign — one of the strongest seasons in league history, featuring a 12-2 record, 1,653 points scored, and a staggering +360 point differential. With nine winning seasons in fourteen years and three separate top-three finishes, the Sattler franchise stands as one of the league's most resilient and consistently competitive organizations.

Franchise Motto Persistence. Evolution. Contention.
