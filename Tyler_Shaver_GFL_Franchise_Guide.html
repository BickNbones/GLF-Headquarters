Postseason Analysis

REGULAR SEASON KING, PLAYOFF PUZZLE

The numbers don't lie — Tyler Shaver's floor is elite. But the gap between his regular season dominance and playoff results reveals an interesting pattern. In 2022, he went 10-4 — the best regular season record of his career — and walked away with nothing.

His 3 championships came in seasons with records of 9-4, 9-4, and 8-6 — proving he peaks when it matters most, but doesn't always survive the bracket. Three rings say he's figured out the playoffs when it counts. One 10-4 season with a 4th-place finish says the bracket is never guaranteed.
