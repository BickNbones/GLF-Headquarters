The Big Ones

Three Shots, Two Rings — One Robbery

Three championship appearances. Two crowns. One soul-crushing robbery. This is the story of a franchise that wins big — and got cheated out of winning bigger.

👑

2014 — The Crown

Champion ⭐ All-Time PF Record

Record: 11-2  ·  PF: 1,590.5 ⭐  ·  Team: CUNT

Won the GFL Championship in Year 1. CUNT was untouchable. The debut was historic — not just for the title, but for setting what may be the all-time GFL single-season scoring record at 1,590.5 points. The kind of first season that makes people suspicious — did Tavis sell his soul? Did he have insider information from the fantasy gods? Nobody knows. What we know is that CUNT walked in, slapped the league around, and walked out with the trophy and the record book. Peak audacity.

🚨

2019 — The Robbery

Runner-Up

Record: 11-2  ·  PF: 1,534.5  ·  Team: VaG

Made the championship. The game ended in a tie. Both teams celebrated. Both teams panicked. Then a post-game stat correction handed the trophy to the Tigers. VaG had done everything right. Everything. The stat gods intervened with the cold, indifferent hand of a spreadsheet formula. This was the robbery that kept CUNT from being a 3x champion.

"Robbed by a spreadsheet."

— Everyone who witnessed this atrocity, 2019

Champion

Record: 9-5  ·  PF: 1,557.7  ·  Team: CUNT

Made the championship for the 3rd time — and this time, finished the job. A decade after his first ring, Tavis Tucker claimed Ring #2. The comeback arc was complete. Three championship appearances. Two rings. One robbery that kept him from the three-peat. At this point, the CUNT's championship résumé reads like a Hollywood script — fall from grace, get robbed, rise again, and take the crown. 2x Champion. Undeniable.

Hall of Injustice

Inductee: The 2019 Stat Correction

On the final day of the 2019 GFL Championship, VaG and the Tigers were tied. The game was over. Celebrations were premature. Then, in the cold light of a Tuesday stat correction, the Tigers were awarded fractional points that swung the result. The title changed hands not on the field of competition, but in a database query. Somewhere, a spreadsheet cell updated. Somewhere, Tavis Tucker screamed into the void. Without this robbery, he'd be a 3x champion.

This robbery has been formally recognized by the GFL Franchise Guide Historical Committee.
