📌 Deploying the Hub — New Dedicated Site

  1. Go to app.netlify.com/drop (or netlify.com/drop)
  2. Drag gfl-hub-v4.html onto the drop zone — this creates a brand new project
  3. Rename it something memorable (e.g. gfl-headquarters)
  4. Your hub URL becomes gfl-headquarters.netlify.app
  5. Each franchise guide links back here via a "← GFL HQ" button — request updated guide files to add that link

⚠️ Do NOT drag this into the Redwood Murders project — that is Collin's franchise guide. The hub is a separate, top-level site that all 12 guides link to and from.

GNFL Franchise Lore

The GNFL’s history is filled with rivalries, heartbreaks, miracle seasons, and forgotten classics. These franchise lore cards highlight the defining stories behind each team.

Barton Spartans

  • Early Powerhouse: Top‑5 finishes in consecutive seasons.
  • Ranking Controversy: 2012 #1 finish sparked the league’s first debate.
  • Rivalry: Heavyweight clashes with MountainMen.

Medford MountainMen

  • First Juggernaut: 9‑win 2013 season.
  • Division Titan: Anchored the chaotic Central.
  • Rivalry: Power collisions with Dawgs of War.

Cleveland Dawgs of War

  • Founding Father Franchise: Led by David Wilson.
  • Heavyweight Season: 9–4 in 2013.
  • Family Lore: Dawgs vs Tyranny is the GNFL’s longest rivalry.

Off Boys

  • Playoff Breakthrough: First mid‑tier postseason run.
  • Chaos Engine: Early upsets shaped playoff seeding.
  • Signature Win: Narrow 2013 victories.

Team Amish Rifle

  • Underrated Titan: Strong early records.
  • Underdog Story: Outperformed expectations in 2012.
  • Draft Lore: Tied to an early draft steal.

Team mymagicjohnson

  • Disaster Season: 2–11 record, 0.1538 win rate.
  • Historical Importance: Helped define parity rules.
  • Trivia: Earliest 30+ point blowout loss.

Your Mom

  • Modern Rivalry: Tight games with Space Cadets.
  • Era Flex: Competitive in early and modern eras.
  • Signature Moment: Early upset win.

Space Cadets

  • Return of Kris: Major franchise comeback.
  • Rivalry: Season‑defining clashes with Your Mom.
  • Franchise Lore: Unique disappearance and revival arc.

North Bay Tyranny

  • Collin’s Franchise: One of the GNFL’s most storied arcs.
  • Family Rivalry: Tyranny vs Dawgs of War.
  • Signature Win: First documented victory is an early rivalry moment.
