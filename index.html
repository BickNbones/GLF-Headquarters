🐶 Dawg Bowl Record Book
Formerly the Gonzo Bowl. The consolation final was known as the Gonzo Bowl through 2024 and was renamed the Dawg Bowl in 2025 in honor of David Wilson, a decade-long GFL manager whose impact continues to be felt throughout the league. The winner receives a Dawg Medallion and, under the rule introduced in 2014, earns the first overall pick in the following season's draft.

David Wilson was a GFL manager for a decade. He showed up every year, competed hard, and brought real heart to the league. He never won the championship — but in 2022, his final season, his son Collin did. Some legacies don't need a ring.

📌 GitHub Pages Deployment

  1. Upload index.html and weekly.html to the repository root
  2. Drag gfl-hub-v4.html onto the drop zone — this creates a brand new project
  3. Rename it something memorable (e.g. gfl-headquarters)
  4. Your hub URL becomes gfl-headquarters.netlify.app
  5. Each franchise guide links back here via a "← GFL HQ" button — request updated guide files to add that link

⚠️ Do NOT drag this into the Redwood Murders project — that is Collin's franchise guide. The hub is a separate, top-level site that all 12 guides link to and from.
