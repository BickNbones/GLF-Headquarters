A Decade of Dawgs Football

D 2nd · C ~10th
2013 2nd Place ⚡ DAD WINS

THE ARRIVAL

9-4-0 PF: 1,349.5 PA: 1,250.5 +99.0

David Wilson announced his arrival to the GFL with one of the strongest debut seasons in league history. A 9-4 record, the 2nd-best finish in the league, and a healthy +99 point differential. The Dawgs weren't just barking — they were biting. The father had arrived.

⚡
D 4th · C 9th
2014 4th Place ⚡ DAD WINS

STILL HUNGRY

9-4-0 PF: 1,316.0 PA: 1,239.5 +76.5

Another 9-4 campaign, another dominant regular season — but this time the playoff bracket had other ideas. David slid to 4th, proving that consistency and success aren't always the same thing in fantasy football. While son Collin (9th) watched from the bottom half, Dad was still very much in the conversation.

⚡
D 7th · C 9th
2015 7th Place ⚡ DAD WINS

GROWING PAINS

6-7-0 PF: 1,291.0 PA: 1,297.0 -6.0

A dip in the standings — 6-7, 7th place — but the numbers tell a tighter story. Just -6 points across 13 weeks of fantasy football. The Dawgs were as competitive as ever; luck just wasn't riding shotgun. Still beat the kid in the standings. Dad doesn't lose sleep.

⚡
D 7th · C 4th
2016 7th Place 👑 SON WINS

THE STUDENT RISES

6-7-0 PF: 1,252.0 PA: 1,256.5 -4.5

Back-to-back 6-7 seasons, and back-to-back 7th place finishes. But something quietly seismic happened across the Wilson household this year — Collin climbed to 4th. For the first time in league history, the son outranked the father. We can only imagine the dinner table conversation.

⚡
D 7th · C 10th
2017 7th Place ⚡ DAD WINS

REBUILDING SEASON

5-8-0 PF: 1,107.5 PA: 1,188.5 -81.0

The roughest statistical year of the David Wilson era. A 5-8 record and -81 point differential painted a bleak picture. But true competitors don't quit — they recalibrate. Collin tanked to 10th, so Dad still held the family bragging rights. Pride, at least, was intact.

⚡
D 6th · C 11th
2018 6th Place ⚡ DAD WINS

THE GRINDER

7-6-0 PF: 1,388.0 PA: 1,442.5 -54.5

Back above .500. 1,388 points scored — the Dawgs were generating offense. The defense was leaky, and 6th place was the ceiling this year, but the fight was back. Meanwhile Collin (11th) hit rock bottom. Dad outpaced the kid by five positions. The master and the student.

⚡
D 3rd · C 7th
2019 3rd Place ⚡ DAD WINS

THE RESURRECTION

7-5-1 PF: 1,361.5 PA: 1,181.0 +180.5

If there was one season that defined David Wilson's brilliance as a fantasy manager, this was it. A +180.5 point differential — by far the biggest of his career — and a 3rd-place finish. The Dawgs were on fire. Collin (7th) looked on from afar. Dad still had it. He always had it.

⚡
D 9th · C 10th
2020 9th Place ⚡ DAD WINS

THE PANDEMIC DIP

6-7-0 PF: 1,391.5 PA: 1,330.0 +61.5

A bizarre, unpredictable NFL season made for a bizarre GFL season. David slipped to 9th — his worst-ever finish — despite a positive point differential. The Dawgs were scoring; they just couldn't catch the breaks. Collin (10th) managed to finish one spot worse. Dad's dignity, somehow, survived.

⚡
D 2nd · C 7th
2021 2nd Place ⚡ DAD WINS

THE RENAISSANCE

8-6-0 PF: 1,500.0 PA: 1,431.0 +69.0

This was David Wilson's masterpiece season. 1,500 points — the only time in his career he cracked that barrier. An 8-6 record. A 2nd-place finish. Playing some of the most dominant fantasy football of his entire decade in the GFL. Collin (7th) watched his father light up the league. The old man still had fire in his belly.

⚡
🕊️ FINAL SEASON
D 6th · C 1st 🏆
2022 6th Place 👑 SON WINS

THE FINAL CHAPTER

6-8-0 PF: 1,481.82 PA: 1,547.48 -65.66

Nobody knew it would be the last one. The 2022 Dawgs were still generating nearly 1,500 points — still competing, still fighting. But across the Wilson family living room, something extraordinary was unfolding. Collin — the son who had spent a decade chasing his father's shadow — won the entire league. 1st place. GFL Champion. In David's final season, the student became the master. There is no more beautiful sendoff a fantasy football father could ever receive. The torch was passed. The legacy lives on. 🐾⚡

"He taught you the game. You won it for him."
