Warning Signs

THE FLINT TROPICS PROBLEM

The rebranding was supposed to signal a new era. 2023 was electric — 11-3, the best record in the modern era — but a 4th place finish exposed the playoff vulnerability. Since then, the numbers tell a different story. PF has dropped from 1,471 (2023) → 1,415 (2024) → 1,246 (2025). That 2025 total is the lowest output since the 2020 collapse. The question isn't whether Steve Davis knows how to build a winner — he has the rings to prove it. The question is whether Flint Tropics can rediscover what Atlas X-Men had.
