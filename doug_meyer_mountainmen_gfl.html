CHAPTERS ON THE TRAIL

2013 🏆

Summit Year One

In the very first season of the Gandorf National Football League, Doug Meyer's Medford MountainMen stood alone at the top. A 9-4-0 record, 1,473.5 points, and a +221.0 point differential. The inaugural GFL champion set the standard in year one.

9-4-0 1st Place PF: 1,473.5 Diff: +221.0
2014–2015

Into the Woods

While others claimed the mountain in 2014 and 2015, Doug was deep in the wilderness — falling to 11th before posting just 4 wins in 2015. Seventy-nine roster moves that year told the whole story — he was hunting every inch of the mountain for an edge and finding only more valley.

11-15-0 combined 11th & 8th 79 moves in 2015
2016 🏆

The Second Summit

Three years after claiming the inaugural title, Doug climbed back to the top. A 10-3-0 season and his second GFL championship — proving the 2013 run was no fluke.

10-3-0 1st Place PF: 1,411.0 Diff: +180.0
2017–2021

Five Years Without a Summit

Five consecutive seasons finishing 8th or 9th. The lone exception was 2018 — a jaw-dropping 12-1-0 record, best in the entire GFL — but the playoff gods denied the ring. What followed was four seasons of grind: 6-7, 6-7, 6-8. Middling finishes for a man who had already stood twice at the top.

36-30-0 combined Highlight: 2018 (12-1-0, 2nd)
2022–2025

Chasing the Ridge

A top-5 return in 2022 (8-6-0, 5th, 1,502 points) hinted at revival. Then came three straight tough seasons — 12th in 2023 (4-10-0), 6th in 2024 (3-11-0), and 11th in 2025 (6-8-0). The mountain is calling, but the summit has been elusive since 2016.

2022: 5th (8-6) 2023: 12th (4-10) 2024: 6th (3-11) 2025: 11th (6-8)
